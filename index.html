Undergraduate AI Literacy Rutgers learning experience

AI Literacy Notebook For Rutgers Undergraduates

A five-module interactive curriculum grounded in Rutgers AI guidance, SAS Core goals, Google AI tools, and library standards
Format: self-paced module, class activity, workshop, or first-year seminar

Learning Architecture

Students learn AI by practicing judgment: choosing tools, grounding claims in sources, protecting data, documenting AI assistance, and reflecting on learning.

Pathway 5

Foundational modules that can be used as a mini-course or embedded across Core courses.

Practice 8

Major-based examples for writing, science, history, policy, data, media, and research.

Tools 4

Gemini, NotebookLM, practice tests, and Classroom workflows treated as learning supports.

Library 1

Library studio for source evaluation, copyright, fair use, citation, and research integrity.

Five Undergraduate Modules
